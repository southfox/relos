# 🕰️ Relós – El Despertador con Sentido del Humor

📘 This README is in English. [Leer en español](README.md)

> The alarm that wakes you up... but with style (and maybe sarcasm).

**Relós** is an alarm clock that mixes sounds, ridiculous phrases, and a theatrical vibe to make sure your mornings are *not boring*. It doesn’t just wake you up — it greets you with an existential giggle.

## 🌟 Features

- ⏰ **Funny alarms**: Random absurd phrases that hit harder than your snooze button.
- 🎭 **Absurd theatrical aesthetic**: Formal, ridiculous, and dressed for the occasion.
- 📱 **iPhone app**: Made for your nightstand or chaotic commute.
- 📲 **Apple Watch support**: Snooze from your wrist while being gently insulted.
- 💻 **macOS (optional)**: Desktop version for dramatic Pomodoro breaks.
- 👁️ **visionOS (experimental)**: Floating nonsense in your immersive workspace. Yes, this is real.

## ❓ Frequently Unasked Questions

> **Does Relós make me more productive?**  
> Depends. Does oversleeping and being late count as productivity?  

> **Can I use it without waking up?**  
> Technically yes. But we wouldn’t recommend it. Or admit it.  

> **Is it safe for children?**  
> Only the sarcastic ones.  

> **Why is it called Relós?**  
> Because “Clock” was too boring. And we weren’t legally allowed to name it “Death-o-Matic 3000.”
